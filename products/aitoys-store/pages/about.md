# About AI Toys Fun

**Toys have always had personality — now they can show it.**

aitoys.fun is a curated toy store with one idea at its heart: the newest generation of toys can *listen, respond, and grow with your child* — telling a bedtime story that stars them, cheering them through their first read-aloud book, or turning a scribble into a drawing lesson.

We hunt for the AI toys that get it right: **fun first, learning built in, privacy non-negotiable.** No endless screens. No toys that talk *at* kids. Just play that plays back.

Every product page tells you three things straight:
1. **What it does** — in plain words, no buzzwords
2. **What your child gets out of it** — the skill hiding inside the fun
3. **What it hears and keeps** — the privacy facts, up front

Started by a builder who believes AI's best use case might just be making a 6-year-old laugh while accidentally learning to read.

*Questions? We answer everything — see Our Parent Promise.*
