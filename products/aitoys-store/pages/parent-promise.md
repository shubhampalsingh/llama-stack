# Our Parent Promise

AI toys are new, and it's right to have questions. Here's exactly where we stand.

## 🔒 Privacy comes first
Every toy we stock is chosen for its privacy design. We prefer toys with **no cameras**, on-device processing where possible, and conversations that are **not stored** or are auto-deleted. Each product page states plainly what the toy hears, processes, and keeps.

## 👪 You're in control
Parent controls aren't a buried setting — they're a requirement to be sold here. Volume caps, time limits, topic filters, and progress visibility: if a toy can't give parents the wheel, we don't stock it.

## 🧠 Play that gives back
We favor toys that trade screen time for **spoken, hands-on, imaginative play** — reading confidence, curiosity, coding logic, emotional vocabulary. Fun first; the learning sneaks in.

## 🛡️ Age-appropriate, always
Every toy is reviewed for the age range on its box — the content it produces, not just its choking-hazard rating. Kid-safe content filters are table stakes.

## 💬 Talk to us
Unsure whether a toy suits your child? Write to us before you buy — real humans, honest answers, including "this one isn't right for your situation."

*AI should make childhood more curious, more giggly, and more confident — never less safe. That's the standard, and we hold every product on this site to it.*
